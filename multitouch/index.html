MultiTouch

A pocket set of tools, scorers, arcade games, board games, puzzles and party games on one round screen: 55 modes, played with taps, swipes, drags round the rim, tilting, shaking and knocking. On the 1.75" AMOLED boards, five music modes join them: pads, a sequencer, a synth, a motion instrument and a sampler. And two AMOLED boards can play together: four games made for two boards, and 19 of the games and scorers on two boards at once.

What you need

  • One of these Waveshare round touch boards:
    • ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75C: 1.75" AMOLED with a speaker and microphones. Every mode, music and the Sampler included.
    • ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75: 1.75" AMOLED. Every mode and the music, but it needs a small speaker plugged into its socket to make sound, and it has no microphones for the Sampler.
    • ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.28: 1.28" LCD. The 55 modes without music (it has no speaker).
  • A USB-C cable that carries data, for installing and charging.
  • Optional: a 3.7 V LiPo battery so it can go anywhere. Check its connector and polarity against the board before plugging it in: they aren't standard.

Install

  1. Plug the board into this computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Press Install under your board, pick the board in the list, then Connect.
  3. Wait for it to finish (a minute or two), then unplug the board and plug it back in to start it.

Install the build for your board: the 1.75C and the 1.75 use different pins, so the other one's build won't work properly (on a 1.75C, the 1.75 build has no sound).

The installer asks whether to Erase device first. Leave it unticked to update and keep your settings, best scores, songs and recordings. Tick it to clear the board completely and start fresh (everything on it is lost, so save a backup from the web setup page first if you want to keep anything). The first install on a 1.75C that had other firmware clears its file area either way.

If the board doesn't show up in the list, it may be asleep: hold its BOOT button while plugging it in, then try again. More in Help.

How to use it

Getting around

Open a groupThe home menu has the groups round the ring (Tools, Score, Arcade, Games, Puzzles, Party, and Music on the AMOLED boards) plus Setup. Tap a group, then tap a mode to start it.
More modesA group with more than 12 modes has a second page: tap > at the end of the ring, and < to come back.
HoldOpens the mode's options: its own settings, ? (how to play), Start again and Exit.
Change a valueDrag round the rim, or use the − and + buttons.
Go backIn the menus, tap the centre button or swipe right. In a mode, swipe right to leave (in a game, only while it's paused), or hold and choose Exit.

Tilt games

The first time you play a tilt game (Maze, Snake, Tilt marble, Balance, and Ring Pong or Rim defender with tilt on), it asks you to lay the device flat and tap. That calibrates the motion sensor. You can do it again with Calibrate in the game's options. If tilting feels the wrong way round, reverse it in Setup.

Setup

Setup, on the home menu, has brightness, the sleep and deep-sleep times, travel mode, screen rotation, tilt sensitivity and direction, clearing best scores, reset and Web setup.

The web page (Wi-Fi)

  1. Go to Setup > Web setup > Start. MultiTouch starts its own Wi-Fi network and shows a QR code, the network name and the password.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone to join it. The page usually opens by itself. If it doesn't, go to http://192.168.4.1.
  3. On the page you can change every setting, hide or reorder modes, upload your own Charades word lists, see and clear best scores, back up and restore everything, and check the battery. On the AMOLED boards, a Music tab swaps the pads' sounds for WAV files.

Tap Stop when you're done. It also stops by itself after 10 minutes without use.

Sleep and wake

  • The centre button on the home menu puts it to sleep. It also sleeps by itself after the Sleep after time.
  • Touch it or pick it up to wake it.
  • After a long sleep it goes into deep sleep to save the battery. A touch still wakes it (and movement, unless travel mode is on). It doesn't deep-sleep while charging or while a timer runs.
  • Travel mode (in Setup) means only a double tap wakes it, so it stays asleep in a bag.
  • A running Pomodoro or Timer wakes it when time is up. While music plays, it stays awake.

The modes

Tools

PomodoroA work and break timer. Tap to start or pause, and swipe left to skip to the next phase. It wakes the device when a phase ends.
Game clockA two-player chess-style clock. Tap your half of the screen when you've moved. You can add time to each move.
DecideFlip a coin (tap or shake), or flick a wheel of 2 to 8 segments.
LevelA spirit level. Tap to freeze the reading, and use "Zero here" in its options.
OracleAsk a yes or no question, then shake for the answer.
TimerA countdown (drag round the rim to set it; it wakes the device when it ends) or a stopwatch with laps.

Score

SnookerTap each ball as it's potted (only the balls you can play are lit). It shows the break, the points left and any snookers needed. A swipe ends a visit, and a swipe down undoes.
DartsPick single, double or treble, then tap the number on the rim. It plays 501 or 301 with checkout hints, Cricket, Round the clock and Points.
PointsA two-player counter. Tap a side to score, with a target and "win by two" if you want them.
CardsRunning totals for 2 to 6 players. Tap a player, set the points on the rim, then tick.
Five diceThe five-dice scorecard game for 1 or 2 players. You get three rolls a turn, and tap dice to hold them.

Arcade

SnakeTilt (or swipe) to steer the snake to its food. Rocks appear as it grows.
MazeTilt to roll the ball to the green exit before time runs out. Each maze is new and each level bigger.
GlideTouch to flap up through the gaps.
Ring PongDrag the paddle round the rim to bounce the ball into the blocks. Tap the middle to pause.
ReflexWait for green, then tap as fast as you can. Five goes, then your average.
OrbitYour ship circles on two tracks. Tap (or knock, if you turn that on) to hop between them and dodge the rocks.
StackTap to drop each sliding slab on the tower. Whatever overhangs is cut off.
WhackTap the moles before they duck down, for 30 seconds.
Tilt marbleTilt to roll the marble over every gem without falling in a hole. You have three lives.
Rim defenderDrag (or tilt) the shield round the rim to block rocks drifting out from the middle.
BalanceTilt to keep the ball on a tipping beam. The beam gets shorter every 10 seconds.
Beat swipeSwipe the way each arrow points as it reaches the middle, on the beat of a tune. Three tracks; you only hear the tune on the AMOLED boards.

Games

EchoA colour-pattern memory game. Watch, then repeat it.
X & ONoughts and crosses against the device (easy, fair or perfect) or a friend.
DiceRoll 1 to 5 dice, from d4 to d20. Tap or shake to roll.
GemsMatch three: swap neighbouring gems to make lines, with 30 moves a game.
MergeThe 2048-style sliding number game, played with swipes.
MinesClear the round minefield. Tap to open a cell, and hold to flag it.
PairsFind the eight pairs in as few moves as you can.
Quick mathsAs many sums as you can in 60 seconds.
Colour clashTap the colour of the ink, not the colour the word says.
Four in a rowTap a column to drop a disc, and get four in a line. Play the device or a friend.
MancalaThe traditional six-pit sowing game, round the ring. Play the device or a friend.
Higher or lowerGuess whether the next card is higher (tap the top half) or lower (the bottom half).

Puzzles

Lights OutTurn every light off. Each tap flips a light and its neighbours.
SlideThe 3x3 or 4x4 sliding-tile puzzle.
Code breakerFind the hidden code of four in ten guesses.
PegsPeg solitaire on the cross-shaped board.
TowerMove the stack to the right-hand peg, never putting a bigger disc on a smaller one.
Number orderRemember where the numbers were, then tap them in order.
FloodTap colours on the rim to grow your blob from the corner, and fill the board before the moves run out.
PipesTurn the tiles until every pipe joins the source in the middle with no open ends.
Mini sudoku4x4 or 6x6 sudoku. Tap a square, then a number.
Binary gridFill the grid with gold and blue: no three in a line, and the same number of each in every row and column.
BridgesJoin the numbered islands with that many bridges each.
Light pathsTurn the mirrors to steer a laser through every target.

Party

Reaction duelTwo players, one half each. The first to touch on green wins the round.
CharadesHold it to your forehead while friends give clues. Tip it down for correct, or up to pass. You can upload your own word lists on the web page.
Hot potatoTap to light a hidden fuse, then pass it round until it goes off.
Tap raceTwo players, one half each. Most taps in 10 seconds wins.
Shake-off2 to 6 players take turns. Most shakes in 10 seconds wins.
Steady hand2 to 6 players take turns keeping a bubble in a shrinking circle.
Spin the bottleTap, shake or flick to spin, with numbered seats round the rim if you want them.
Guess the time2 to 6 players take turns. Start a hidden clock, then stop it when you think the target time is up.

Music (1.75" AMOLED boards only)

All five music modes share one layout: the top arc switches between them (the music keeps playing; tap the one you're in for its options), the middle panel has the mode's own controls, and the bottom arc has REC, play / pause and the pattern. A swipe right goes back a page.

Pads16 sounds on pads round the rim, played the moment your finger lands. Choose a built-in kit or one of your own. With REC on, your hits are recorded into the pattern.
SequencerOne bar of 16 steps round the ring. Tap steps to place the sound you last played, slide from a step to change its note, chain patterns into a song, and hold FX for loops, filters and more.
SynthTouch notes round the rim and slide to glide between them. Change the sound, octave and scale in the middle. Swipe left for an X/Y filter pad.
MotionPlay by moving the device: tilt for the note and its tone, and shake or knock to play drums.
Sampler1.75C only. Record up to 30 seconds with the built-in microphones, trim it, chop it into slices and put them on pads.

Two boards (1.75" AMOLED boards only)

Two AMOLED boards running MultiTouch link up directly by radio, a few metres apart. No Wi-Fi network or router is needed.

  1. Pair them once: open Duo > Pair on both and knock them together edge to edge, or tap the other board's name in the list. They stay paired after a restart. Pair also shows whether the other board is in reach, and has Ping it, Turn the gate and Unpair.
  2. Play: open a Duo game, or choose Two boards in a mode's options (hold for the options). The other board opens the same thing and asks: tap to join, hold for no thanks. One board in the options goes back to playing alone.

The Duo group

Quick DrawWait for DRAW!, then the first to flick their wrist wins. Too soon loses.
Blind MazeOne rolls an orb through a maze they can't see; the other sees the maze and talks them to the middle.
Star FeederSwipe stars out through the gate; the other board catches them in a basket it tilts. Dodge the red ones.
Defuse DuoOne board has the bomb, the other has the rules. Talk it through before the clock runs out.

Two boards in other modes

A player eachGame clock (a clock each), X & O, Four in a row, Mancala (your side at the bottom on each board), Five dice, Reaction duel, Tap race, and Hot potato (hold it a moment, then tap or swipe to throw it to the other board).
RacesHigher or lower, Quick maths, Colour clash, Echo, Shake-off, Steady hand and Guess the time: both boards get the same cards, sums or pattern and start together, and the scores are compared at the end.
One scoreboardPoints, Snooker, Darts and Cards show the same score on both boards. Either can score, and undo works on either.

The board that asked is the host, and its settings are used. If the other board goes out of reach, the game waits; after 8 seconds both give up.
